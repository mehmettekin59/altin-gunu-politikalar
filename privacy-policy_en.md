Privacy Policy
Last Updated: May 15, 2025
This Privacy Policy explains how the Gold Day application (hereinafter referred to as the "Application") protects users' privacy and how it collects, uses, stores, and shares user data.
Before using the Application, please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the Application, you accept the practices described in this policy.
1. Information We Collect
Our application collects and stores the following information:
1.1 Information Provided by the User

Participant Information: Participant names you enter for your Gold Day organization
Organization Information: Organization details such as Gold Day duration, start date, monthly amount
Raffle Results: Results of raffles conducted within the Application

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

User Preferences: Your language preference (Turkish, English, Arabic)
Application Settings: In-app settings such as data update frequency

1.3 Information We Do Not Collect

Location information
Camera or microphone access
Phone contacts
Identity or financial information
Social media accounts
Data belonging to other applications

2. Use of Information
The information we collect is used for the following purposes:

Helping you manage your Gold Day organization
Conducting raffle processes
Storing and applying your application settings
Providing current information about gold and currency exchange rates
Improving application performance and user experience

3. Data Storage and Security
3.1 Data Storage

All user data is stored locally on your device (using Android DataStore)
Your data is stored until you choose to delete it or until you uninstall the application

3.2 Data Security

Storing data only on your device enhances security
Appropriate technical measures have been taken to protect your data
PDF file creation and sharing processes are carried out securely

4. Third-Party Services
4.1 API Usage
Our application connects to the Kapali Carsi API to provide information about gold and currency exchange rates. During this API usage:

Your personal data is not sent to the API provider
All communication with the API is conducted through secure connections (HTTPS)

4.2 Google Play Services
When you download the Application from the Google Play Store, Google Play Services' own data collection policies may apply. For more information about these, you can review the Google Privacy Policy.
5. Children's Privacy
Our application does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have collected personal information from a child under 13, please contact us, and we will delete this information from our systems.
6. User Rights
As a user, you have the following rights:

Right of Access: You can access your data stored in the application
Right of Correction: You can correct your incorrect or incomplete data
Right of Deletion: You can delete your data within the application or delete all your data by uninstalling the application
Data Portability: You can export and share your raffle results as PDF files

7. Changes to the Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When any changes are made, we will publish a notification within the application and/or publish the updated policy on this page. Continuing to use the application after changes means you accept the updated policy.
